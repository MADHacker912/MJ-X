## 2. Short Description (For Product Hunt Overview)

> MJ is a full-stack, local-first multimodal autonomous AI operating environment. Powered by the bidirectional Google Gemini Live API with automatic 1011 fault-tolerant recovery, MJ sees your screen, listens to your voice with sub-second latency, executes complex OS workflows, manages its own SQLite WAL long-term memory, and links your desktop and Android devices — with zero subscription fees and 100% data privacy.

## 3. Detailed Product Pitch (Description / What it does)

```markdown
### Meet MJ — Your Autonomous Multimodal Desktop Co-Pilot

Standard AI chatbots are trapped inside browser tabs without true system integration. MJ changes that: it lives on your desktop, perceives what you are doing, communicates through fluid bidirectional voice, and autonomously executes approved system actions.

### ⚡ Key Capabilities:
- 🎙️ **Real-Time Bidirectional Voice**: Powered by Google Gemini Live WebSocket API with sub-second latency and custom fault-tolerant 1011 auto-reconnection with backoff jitter.
- 🧠 **Cortex Local-First Memory Engine**: SQLite WAL architecture with FTS5 semantic search and Hinglish phonetic normalization. Your data stays on your device.
- 🎭 **Echo Affective Intelligence**: 5D emotion tracking (valence, arousal, dominance) with realistic emotional inertia and decay.
- 👁️ **Desktop Perception & Vision**: Real-time screen analysis, IDE debugging assistance, and OS automation.
- 🛡️ **P0 Security Vault & Sandbox**: OS Keychain credential isolation, subprocess sandboxing, and immediate emergency stop.
- 📱 **PyQt6 Cyberpunk HUD & Android Companion**: Beautiful desktop overlay, FastAPI telemetry dashboard, and phone synchronization.
- 🧪 **187/187 Automated Tests**: Zero mocks, production-grade reliability verified with 100% pass rate.
```
